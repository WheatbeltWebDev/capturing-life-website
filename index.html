About

At home in the country

I'm a Wheatbelt photographer with a deep love for farming, animals and the country way of life. I grew up surrounded by open paddocks, working dogs, horses and big skies, and that's still where I feel most at home behind the lens.

My photography is about honest, unhurried moments: the first light over a crop, a horse at rest, a farmer at the end of a long day, or the coast at golden hour after a week on the land. Whether it's your farm, your animals or your family, I'd love to capture the life you've built.
